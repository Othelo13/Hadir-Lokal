# P3.2 — Historical daily recap

historicalDailyRecap merges P3.1 evidence with member and schedule snapshots. Read only; the API must wrap the entire read in one SQLite snapshot. The server supplies the clock; no client-supplied as-of instant.

Policy: use the last observed instant of the selected local calendar day (end of a past day, current instant for today). Schedule intervals use their own historical timezone, not current settings. When the timezone changes within a day, select the latest observed interval, report timezoneChanged and mark coverage partial. Same-timestamp changes use event_id. If the schedule is deleted, do not infer roster. Only active employees at that snapshot on a working day receive empty rows; evidence remains regardless of eligibility, account deletion, role change or schedule. Snapshot identity wins when the member exists; otherwise use stored evidence identity. A member who left on a day without evidence is not included if ineligible at end of that day. No invented precoverage membership and no projected future roster; approved future absence remains explicit evidence.

Context coverage: complete means history covers the start of the observed local day and there is no intraday timezone ambiguity/deleted schedule; partial includes migration/first schedule mid-day or timezone changes; unknown means no known schedule interval for that day; future means beyond current known office date without observed interval. Complete for today does not mean the day has finished. Missing roster data remains distinct from a known nonworking day. Event timezone is taken from schedule history at its timestamp, or null before coverage. Stored local dates, presence status and absence effective dates are never recalculated. This is a current recap of recorded evidence, not a reconstruction of approval/review status as it existed at the historical date.

No new migrations or HTTP endpoints. P3.3 adds authorized date/filter/pagination API and P3.4 detail. UI must disclose partial/unknown coverage and avoid displaying inferred historical timezone as known. Tests use synthetic histories and a fixed clock.
